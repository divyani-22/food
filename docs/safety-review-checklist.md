# Safety review checklist (for a qualified human reviewer)

Prepared as a first-pass audit. These rules were written from model memory. They are NOT clinically validated and NOT a substitute for review by a qualified food-safety professional (shelf life, storage, rescue, ethylene) and a clinician or pharmacist (medication, condition, allergen rules).

How to read: "Source" says what was checked. "[secondary]" means a web search summary or news/clinic page quoting the authority, not the primary authority page (the primary sites could not be fetched from this environment). "unsourced" means from model memory only. "Confidence" is the auditor's confidence the value is not unsafe, not a measure of clinical correctness. Tick when you have verified the line; use Notes for corrections.

Companion document: docs/safety-rules-review.md (what was changed and why).

## What this review does NOT cover

- Clinical validation of any medication, condition or dose statement. No rule has been checked by a clinician or pharmacist.
- Individual circumstances: doses, kidney function, INR, allergy severity, pregnancy stage, immune status, infants and young children.
- Country-specific regulation and labelling (EU/UK/US/India), regional food practice, and local climate (ambient temperature, power cuts, humidity).
- Product-specific recipe changes, cross-contact or "may contain" statements, and OCR/ingredient-parsing errors upstream.
- Home canning, fermentation, sous vide, infant formula and baby food, raw-milk products, wild foods.
- Whether UI wording shows the disclaimer everywhere it should (see the review log for UI notes).
- Primary-source verification: the primary authority pages (USDA FSIS, FDA, NHS, UC Davis) could not be fetched in this environment; checks used web-search summaries and are labelled [secondary].
